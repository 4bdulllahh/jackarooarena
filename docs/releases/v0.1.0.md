## JackarooArena 0.1.0: play Jackaroo online with your friends

The first public version. Make a room, send the link to three friends (or let bots fill the
empty seats) and play 2 against 2 around a low table in a warm 3D majlis, right in the browser.

### Playing together

- **Rooms with a 4-letter code.** Create a room, share the code or link, pick your seat and team. The host starts the game.
- **Bots fill empty seats.** Play with two friends, one friend or alone. If someone takes longer than 15 seconds, a bot plays that turn for them.
- **Rejoin after a dropped connection.** Reload the page or open the link again and you're back in your seat.
- **Rematch.** When a team wins, the host can start a new game in the same room.

### The game

- **Upgraded Levant house rules.** Every card works the way the house plays it: the 7 split, the 5 that moves any marble, red 10, J and Q tricks, the K that kills everything in its path, safe start tiles and burning cards.
- **Nobody can cheat.** The server deals and keeps every hand hidden, and checks every move.
- **Play with the mouse or the keyboard.** Click a card, then a glowing marble, then where it should go. Number keys pick cards, and every option is also in a list on the side.
- **Taunts.** Press T for six quick taunts with sounds and speech bubbles. Bots gloat after a kill.

### The majlis

- **A cosy 3D room.** Floor cushions, a carpet, carved wall niches, a brass chandelier and lanterns, with warm light and real shadows. A soft retro TV filter gives it the old console feel.
- **Your phone is the menu.** You hold a phone in one hand and a glass of tea in the other. Play, How to play and Settings are on its screen, and Esc brings it back up during a game.
- **How to play.** Eight short pages on the phone explain the rules.
- **Settings.** Separate volume sliders for everything, game sounds and taunts, and Low, Medium or High graphics for slower or faster computers. M mutes, V switches to a top-down view of the board.
